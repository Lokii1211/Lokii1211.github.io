SmartDetect — Enterprise Real-Time Multi-Camera Person Tracking & Re-ID

An enterprise-grade video analytics and re-identification platform that detects, registers, and tracks individuals across heterogeneous camera feeds (live webcams, RTSP streams, and surveillance video uploads). Automatically assigns individuals persistent SDT-XXXX identifiers, building chronological movement trails with photo evidence and DPDP/GDPR data governance.

5-Stage Identity Arbitration Cascade: Combines YOLOv8n resolution-aware detection (416/640/960px) → ByteTrack multi-object tracking with ID-switch protection → InsightFace ArcFace (512-dim face anchor, ≥0.56) → OSNet x1.0 (512-dim body Re-ID) → Face-Anchor Principle that permanently blocks fallback contamination on stranger faces.
Deep Learning YOLOv8n (Ultralytics), ByteTrack (supervision), InsightFace (ArcFace buffalo_l 512-d), OSNet x1.0 Re-ID
Arbitration Modules Sliced/Tiled High-Res Detection, 5-point Head-Pose Gate, Hungarian Optimal Face Assignment (SciPy)

Dashboard (8 Routes) React 18.3.1 + Vite, 4-Stream CCTV Grid, Reverse Face Photo Search, Carried Object Feed, Audit Logs
